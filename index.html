03 / WHAT’S GROWING

Our First Chapter

Every story grows at its own pace. Select a pink water drop to see where each game stands.

Character development is underway, with ten base survivor models prepared and the first four characters prioritized for detailed production.

Current development focuses on establishing the character creation workflow, improving the Hana Hospital environment, and building the foundational gameplay systems.

Shawn’s storyline is currently undergoing review and rewrites to create the most engaging and enjoyable experience possible.

The game’s narrative is taking shape, but development of its visual world has not yet begun.

Base One: Ascend is currently in early pre-production. Its core story, characters, gameplay mechanics, and branching-choice system have been established.

Development has not yet progressed to a fully playable prototype.

Hana Petals Game Studio is growing its first three original worlds. Studio-wide announcements will appear here as they're ready.
